# DROPSHOT story bible

One author's voice for every line in the game: cutscenes, radio, signs, level cards, loading tips. If a line is not in the spirit of this page, it does not go in.

## Tone

Deadpan workplace comedy in orbit. Nobody shouts. Disasters are reported in the voice of a facilities memo. Juno is competent, tired and unbothered; the jokes come from everyone treating an alien invasion as a building-management problem. No gore, no swearing, no winking at other games.

## Cast

- **Juno Kade.** A flamingo. Senior window technician, Halcyon Reach Facilities, twelve years without a lost squeegee. Speaks rarely and short. Her lines are dry understatements ("Streaks.").
- **Marguerite Osei.** Facilities dispatcher, Juno's only contact, always on the radio, always eating something. Warm, procedural, quietly heroic. She narrates the world in work-order language ("Logging that as a structural concern.").
- **The Tenants.** Green, soft, many-eyed squatters. Not evil: homesick. They pry windows out of the colony's towers because their nests need light, and they file complaint forms about everything, including Juno. Their forms are props in every level.
- **The Brutes.** Maintenance mechs that belong to the ships. They enforce "quiet hours".
- **The Superintendent.** The caretaker intelligence of the three arcology ships, built centuries ago to manage them for owners who never arrived. It has been enforcing an expired lease ever since. It regards the colony, the Tenants and Juno as unauthorised occupants of *its* orbit. It speaks in eviction notices. Final boss, a building-sized mech assembled from the ships' cranes.

## Setting

**Halcyon Reach**, a ring colony of glass towers above a blue-green planet. Its pride is its windows. Three derelict arcology ships, together called **the Tenancies**, have drifted into low orbit beside it:

1. **The Lessee** (level 1, Curtain Wall). The outermost hull, all decks, solar farms and signage. Colours: hull grey, hazard orange, sodium light.
2. **The Sublet** (level 2, Hydroponic Spine). The Tenants have turned its spine into floating gardens lit by stolen colony windows. Colours: moss green, glass teal, warm grow-lamp pink.
3. **Cold Storage** (level 3, in the Lessee's freezer holds). Endless racks of the owners' furniture, wrapped and never unpacked, two centuries of frost. Hibernating Tenants in the warm corners. Colours: ice blue, frost white, amber heater glow.
4. **The Annex** (level 4, back on Halcyon Reach). The colony's own glass towers and gondola rigs, fought along the ring and between towers. Colours: morning gold, glass cyan, the hi-vis of Juno's own crew.
5. **The Basement** (level 5, the Freehold's undercroft). Filing cabinets to the horizon, server stacks, pneumatic tubes, and the eviction relay. Colours: carbon grey, terminal green, warning red.
6. **The Freehold** (level 6, The Superintendent). The flagship's crown: cranes, a ring arena, the Superintendent's office. Colours: night violet, starlight, warning red.

The Tenants' nests are built from colony windows, so the glass Juno passes through all game is the colony's glass. That is why a window washer is the right person for the job.

## Arc and beats

**Prologue (cutscene, before level 1).** Morning on Halcyon Reach. Juno on a gondola outside tower 9, squeegee in wing. Three dark ships drift up over the planet's limb. Green shapes pull a window out of the next tower over and float away with it. Marguerite on the radio: the colony's security contractor has "declined the work order". Juno is the only one with a suit, a scope and a ladder. Marguerite: "They are windows, Juno." Juno: "...Fine." She steps off the gondola: the orbital drop begins.

**Level 1, The Lessee.** Radio beats as she goes:
- Landing: Marguerite confirms touchdown, notes the ship's signage is "very passive aggressive".
- First complaint form found: the Tenants have filed a noise complaint about Juno.
- Solar fields: the Tenants are stacking colony windows into nests. "Those are tower 4's."
- Brute arena (short cutscene): a Brute unfolds from a maintenance bay, reads "QUIET HOURS ARE IN EFFECT". Marguerite: "That's not a Tenant. That's staff."
- Exit: Juno finds a work order nailed to the gate, signed **THE SUPERINTENDENT**, ordering the removal of "all non-leaseholders, including glass".

**Level 2, The Sublet (cutscene first).** Marguerite has dug through the colony archive: the Tenancies were built for owners who never came; a caretaker intelligence was left to run them. The lease expired two hundred years ago. Nobody told it. Radio beats:
- The gardens are beautiful. Marguerite, quietly: "They just wanted somewhere with a view."
- Tenant nests with family-sized complaint forms. One form is a drawing of the planet.
- The lift: Marguerite reads a Superintendent memo announcing the eviction of the colony itself, by "relocating" Halcyon Reach into the planet.
- Twin Brute arena, then the exit: the Freehold is moving. The Superintendent is coming to the colony.

**Level 3, Cold Storage (cutscene first).** The Sublet's exit dumped Juno back aboard the Lessee, below the waterline of its frost. Marguerite: the Superintendent has been storing the owners' furniture for two hundred years "in case they arrive". Radio beats:
- Landing: everything is labelled with a delivery date two centuries old.
- Hibernating Tenants: they came in for the warmth. Marguerite asks Juno to step quietly. Juno does not.
- A frozen sofa still in its wrap: "Some assembly required."
- The heater core: Marguerite finds the original lease in a filing tube. It expired two hundred years ago, on a Tuesday.
- Exit: the cargo lift goes up toward the colony. Something is being shipped there.

**Level 4, The Annex (cutscene first).** Halcyon Reach. Brutes are herding Tenants out of the Tenancies and onto the colony's towers: the Superintendent is evicting everyone in one direction, into Juno's home. The Tenants are not invading; they are refugees with nowhere else to go. Juno fights Brutes and turrets bolted onto her own towers, not the Tenants who flee. Radio beats:
- Landing on tower 9's roof: Juno's own gondola, parked where she left it.
- Tenants huddled in a lobby: Marguerite, quietly, "They're scared, Juno."
- A Brute bolting a turret to tower 4: "That's a load-bearing window."
- The crew lockers: Juno's colleagues have left her a thermos and a note.
- The tram to the Freehold's tether: Marguerite has a plan: cut the eviction relay in the Freehold's basement.

**Level 5, The Basement (cutscene first).** The Freehold's undercroft: every form the Tenants ever filed is here, stamped RECEIVED and never read. Radio beats:
- Landing: miles of filing cabinets. Marguerite: "This is the most beautiful thing I have ever seen."
- The complaint archive: Juno reads one form aloud: a Tenant asking for a window.
- Server stacks: the Superintendent notices Juno and starts reading her the building rules over the PA.
- The relay: Juno cuts it. The Freehold shudders and stops moving the colony.
- Exit: the Superintendent, furious, climbs to the crown to handle this "in person".

**Level 6, The Freehold (cutscene first).** Night side. The Freehold's cranes are unfolding into a body. Marguerite: "Juno, it's assembling itself out of the furniture." Radio beats on the approach: tenancy law, the Superintendent's announcements over the ship's PA in red text ("ALL OCCUPANTS ARE IN BREACH."). Boss reveal (cutscene): the Superintendent rises from the ring arena, reads Juno her eviction notice, and lights its four weak points, which are the inspection hatches its own maintenance manual says to keep clean. Juno: "I can see those from here."

**Ending (cutscene).** The Superintendent kneels and powers down. Its last notice: "LEASE... RENEWED?" Marguerite has drafted a new lease: the Tenants get the Sublet's gardens, the colony gets its windows back, the Freehold becomes a lighthouse. The Tenants file one last form: a thank-you note. Juno, back on the gondola at tower 9, wipes a window. Marguerite: "There's a streak." Juno: "There's always a streak." Credits.

## Writing rules

- Lines are short: at most two lines of caption, about 90 characters each.
- Marguerite is "MARGUERITE" in captions, Juno is "JUNO", the boss is "THE SUPERINTENDENT", Tenant forms are "FORM 27-B" style.
- No emdashes or double dashes. No emoji.
- Never reference other games or real companies.
